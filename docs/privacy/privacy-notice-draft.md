# Privacy notice (draft `v0-draft`)

**This is a draft, not legal advice, and not yet reviewed by a data-protection professional. Do not treat this as the live privacy notice for anything beyond internal testing.** Its version string matches `POLICY_VERSION` in `src/lib/server/onboarding.ts`, and `policy_acceptances.version` records which version each player accepted.

---

## Who we are

This app is run by its operator for a private group of friends. [Contact address to be added before real use.]

## What we collect and why

Display name, email, timezone, avatar colour, your optional favourite driver and team, your reminder preference, your picks and answers, your scores, which leagues you're in, your Discord user ID (only if you link it), and a record of when you accepted these terms and confirmed you're 18+.

We use this to run the game: to know who you are, contact you about your leagues, and score your predictions. We don't use it for anything else, and we don't sell or share it with advertisers.

## Who we share it with

The companies that host and run this app for us: currently Netlify (hosting), Neon (database), and Resend (email). See our [vendor register](./vendor-register.md) for the full, up-to-date list. We never share your email or picks with other players before results are locked in, and never share your email with anyone outside these providers.

## How long we keep it

See our [retention schedule](./retention-schedule.md). In short: as long as your account is active, plus some housekeeping periods for things like login links and rate-limit counters.

## Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, correct it, or ask us to delete it. Deleting has two options: we can anonymise your account (your picks stay so league tables still work, but nothing identifies you as the player who made them) or erase everything including your picks. See our [access request runbook](./access-request-runbook.md) for how to ask.

## Age

You must be 18 or over to use this app, because of the side-stakes some leagues use.

## Changes to this notice

If we materially change this notice, we'll ask you to accept the new version the next time you sign in.
