# Terms of use (draft `v0-draft`)

**This is a draft, not legal advice, and not yet reviewed by a professional. Do not treat this as the live terms for anything beyond internal testing.** Its version string matches `POLICY_VERSION` in `src/lib/server/onboarding.ts`, and `policy_acceptances.version` records which version each player accepted.

---

## What this is

An unofficial, invite-only F1 predictions game for a private group. It's not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Formula 1 companies in any way.

## Who can use it

You must be invited by a league owner and be 18 or over. There's no public sign-up.

## The pot

Some leagues may track an informal pot or side-stakes between players, as a private note inside the app. **The app itself never takes, holds, or pays out money.** Any actual money changes hands directly between players, outside the app, entirely at their own arrangement and risk.

## Your account

Keep your sign-in link and session private. If you think your account has been compromised, contact us so we can help secure it.

## Conduct

Play fair. Don't try to manipulate scoring, exploit bugs to your advantage, or harass other players.

## Data

See our [privacy notice](./privacy-notice-draft.md) for what we collect and why.

## Changes

We may update these terms. If we make a material change, you'll be asked to accept the new version.

## Liability

This is a hobby project provided as-is, with no warranty. We're not liable for any losses arising from its use, including any side-stakes arrangements between players, which are entirely their own responsibility.

## Ending your account

You can ask to have your account erased at any time - see the [access request runbook](./access-request-runbook.md). We may remove an account that breaks these terms.
